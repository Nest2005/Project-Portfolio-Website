About me

Fourth-year IT student focused on building reliable, practical systems.

I am Ralph Ernest D. Hulguin, a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Cebu Eastern College, Inc. My coursework has centered on designing and building complete systems in which the interface, the database, and the logic behind them work together.

During my studies, I have developed a food ordering website for Cebu City, a news website covering the conflict between the Philippines and China, a digital logbook for our computer laboratory, and an API that serves student and teacher data in JSON format. Each project strengthened a different skill, from interface design and styling to the way data travels between a server and a screen.

My interests span web development, software development, game development, and network administration. I value understanding how a system works at a fundamental level, and I aim to produce work that is reliable, readable, and useful. After graduation, I intend to begin a career as an IT professional and continue to grow by solving real-world problems.

While my professional compass points firmly toward Network Administration, I deliberately keep my curiosity wide. I am actively exploring Game Development, where logic and creativity converge; Software Development, where structure and problem-solving produce dependable applications; and Web Development, where design and data reach the people who rely on them.

Each discipline sharpens the others, and together they shape a more complete technologist. Yet my main focus remains fixed: to build the knowledge, discipline, and judgment required to design, secure, and sustain the networks on which every one of these fields depends.
